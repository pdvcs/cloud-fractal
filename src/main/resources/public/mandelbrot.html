How to Explore

Desktop Controls
  • Zoom In: Click and drag to create a selection box, then release to zoom into that area
  • Pan: Hold Shift and drag, or right-click and drag to move around
  • Alternative: Click "Pan" button to toggle between zoom and pan modes
  • Undo: Click Undo to go back to the previous view
  • Reset: Return to the initial view
Mobile Controls
  • Pinch Zoom: Use two fingers to pinch in/out to zoom
  • Two-Finger Pan: Keep two fingers same distance apart and drag to move around
  • Zoom Selection: Drag with one finger to create a selection box
  • Pan Mode: Enable "Pan" button to pan with one finger instead of zooming
